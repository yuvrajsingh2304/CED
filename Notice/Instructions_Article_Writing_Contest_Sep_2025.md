# Article Writing Contest – Instructions & Guidelines

Organized by the **Department of Civil Engineering, Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Ludhiana**
*(On the occasion of Engineer’s Day 2025, marking the Platinum Year of Excellence of GNDEC)*

## Theme

**“How Civil Engineering Can Help Restore Flood-Affected Buildings in Punjab?”**

## Guidelines

1. **Eligibility:**

   * Open to all Civil Engineering students (B.Tech. 1st to 4th year & M.Tech. students).

2. **Format of Article:**

   * Word limit: **800 – 1200 words**
   * Language: English
   * File format: PDF 
   * Font: Times New Roman, Size 12, Line spacing 1.5
   * Include:

     * Title of the article
     * Name, Roll No., Year/Class, Department

3. **Originality:**

   * Articles must be original.
   * Plagiarism above **20%** will lead to disqualification.

4. **Submission:**

   * Last date: **15th September 2025 (Monday), 11:59 PM**
   * Submit via Google Form:
     👉 [Click here to submit your article](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdxsw8zC5Uv_rkjCBCtTaIDGQ-VdgzTO8XvDKFCIUOB-P4V6A/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=101221929860157528159)

5. **Evaluation Criteria:**

   * Relevance to theme
   * Innovativeness of ideas
   * Clarity and structure
   * Practical applicability

6. **Results & Recognition:**

   * Top 3 articles will be awarded.
   * All participants will receive e-certificates.
