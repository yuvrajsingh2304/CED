## 🎯 The Challenge <a name="challenge"></a>

Create a **self-explanatory digital poster/infographic** showing a natural-disaster-related challenge and highlighting how **engineering, technology and innovation** can help in **prevention, preparedness, mitigation, response or resilient recovery**.

Your entry should communicate:

**Disaster/Problem → Impact → Engineering Solution → Expected Benefit**

Possible themes include:

* Floods
* Earthquakes
* Landslides
* Cyclones and extreme weather
* Resilient infrastructure
* Early-warning systems
* Artificial Intelligence (AI)
* GIS and remote sensing
* Smart technologies
* Sustainable construction
* Other innovative approaches related to disaster resilience

> **Think beyond the disaster. Show how engineering can build resilience.**

# 🤖 AI & Digital Tools <a name="ai"></a>

## **AI is fully permitted in EDR 2026.**

Participants may use AI and digital tools extensively, including for:

* Ideation and brainstorming
* Content development
* Image generation
* Graphic design
* Data visualization
* Poster/infographic creation
* Complete AI-assisted generation

> **There is no restriction on the extent of AI assistance.**

However, participants are responsible for the **accuracy, relevance, originality and overall quality** of the final submission.

Participants must disclose the major **AI/digital tools used** in the submission form.

> 💡 **AI can create the content. You are responsible for the idea, accuracy and message.**
